# Enrico Deiana

## Why it's here
An individual product designer's portfolio: editorial serif type at massive scale on a dark background. It's useful as a **solo designer** reference for how to frame yourself, and for its big-type project list.

> ⚠️ When I analysed it on 2026-10-19, the page contained **injected spam text** (casino/payment SEO paragraphs) mixed into the hero area. The site looks compromised. Treat it as a visual reference only, and never copy text or code from it.

## Stack (observed)
- **Webflow**, with a custom cursor (`cursor: none` on body). No canvas.
- Fonts: **Grandslang** (a high-contrast display serif, used at about 300px for section words), **Migra**, **Degular Display** (10px uppercase labels, letter-spacing 2px), **Wremena**, Exo.

## Layout & grid
- The hero is centred and stacked:
  - a small label `PRODUCT DESIGNER`
  - `CREATING / DELIGHTFUL` in huge serif caps
  - `experiences` in a large italic serif
  - `for your` small, with `business` bold italic
  - Mixed sizes, cases and italics are combined in one lockup.
- Then a short bio paragraph (credentials: Microsoft Bing team, Awwwards jury) and a circular `PUSH ME! / Explore More` button.
- A `PORTFOLIO • PORTFOLIO •` giant serif marquee as the section divider.
- The project list alternates: a large rounded image card, with the project name in about 60px serif overlapping the image edge and a tiny uppercase discipline list (`ART DIRECTION • WEB DESIGN • 3D`).
- Then a playground section with a marquee of words (`Failing / Dreaming / Talking / Sleeping`).

## Typography
- **Mixed-style lockups:** roman caps, italic lowercase and bold italic together. Expressive, but needs restraint.
- Section words are huge (about 300px) and cropped at the viewport edges.

## Colour
- Background charcoal `#1e2125`, type cream `#f3efe6`. A loader screen in cream.

## Motion & interaction

### Overlapping project title on image
- The serif title sits half over and half below the image card, and parallaxes slightly on scroll.
- **Steal level:** the idea. Overlapping type and image is a nice editorial touch for case-study headers.

### Credentials line in the hero
- A one-sentence "who I am and proof" line under the headline.
- **Steal level:** exact, for my bio. A short, confident line plus my HMI focus.

### Custom cursor, marquees, "PUSH ME!" circle
- **Steal level:** avoid. They're gimmicky, and a hidden system cursor hurts usability.

## What NOT to copy
- The spam text, custom cursor, multiple marquees and emoji CTA (`VIEW ALL PROJECTS 🔥`).

## Notes
Use it for **how a solo designer introduces themselves** and **editorial overlap of title and image**. Nothing structural.
