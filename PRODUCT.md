## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary, now:** people in the design field (designers, studios, mentors, faculty) and anyone curious, looking at Jainam Kapadia's body of work.
- **Primary, later:** employers hiring a designer, first for internships, then full-time roles. They skim fast and leave when a page is a wall of text; the site has to hold them by showing the work clearly and in order.

## Product Purpose

A personal design portfolio that shows all of Jainam's work in one place: UI/UX, graphic design, service design, HMI design, plus sketches and drawings. It also has an About section so visitors meet the person, not only the projects.

Success, near term: internships and recognition in the design field. Later: being hired as a designer. Visitors should be impressed by the work and by how it is presented, and find the site easy to use.

## Positioning

A general design portfolio, like every designer has, made distinct by its presentation and craft. Jainam is a multi-disciplinary designer. Automotive HMI is one interest among several, never the site's identity.

## Operating Context

- Employers spend little time per portfolio, so every project shows its full content, organised systematically: scannable, visual first, with no paragraphs-on-paragraphs.
- The site itself is part of the portfolio: its layout, usability and motion demonstrate skill.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Stack: Astro (static) in `CodeFiles/`, content in Markdown collections, deployed to GitHub Pages at https://jainamkapadia20-sys.github.io/Main_Portfolio/ (`.github/workflows/pages.yml`).
- Discipline categories to support: UI/UX, Graphic design, Service design, HMI design, Sketches & drawings. More projects arrive over time and are added as files.
- Personal car project (Honda City, 3D model, specs) stays a small section later in the site, alongside other skills such as sketching. Never a large section, never the theme.
- Car/automotive motifs may appear only as subtle details. No element that takes half the screen. No random photos of the car.
- Undecided: final project list per category beyond Adaptive Cluster; CV file; About copy beyond the current draft.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Jainam Kapadia, designer (student, open to internships).
- Personality: a designer with broad interests; cars are one interest, not the personality.
- **Visual world (user-pinned, 2026-10-24): keep the simple red-and-black theme with both a light and a dark mode.** Simplicity lives in the look; the complexity and richness belong to the site's flow and its animations. Do not propose alternative visual worlds; refine this one.
- Reference bar: professional studio and designer portfolios (e.g. Studio Freight, Aristide Benoist, Lusion), learned from, not copied.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the hero; the site recedes around it.
2. Show, don't tell: visuals and structure carry each project; text is short and scannable.
3. Breadth over a single niche: every discipline gets an equal, organised place.
4. Easy to use beats clever: a visitor finds any project in one or two moves.
5. The craft of the site is itself proof of skill.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
